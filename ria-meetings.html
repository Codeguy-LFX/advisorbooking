FAQ

Do you sell leads?

No. We book attended first meetings for your firm only. Nothing is shared with another advisor.

We’ve tried agencies before. Why is this different?

Most agencies sell a campaign and report on views. We sell one thing: attended, qualified meetings. You see one number every week, so if it isn’t working you know early, not six months in.

How does compliance work?

Your compliance team approves every message before it is sent. We send from separate domains in your firm’s name and keep a reply log your compliance team can access. The compliance decision stays with your firm.

What stops you from booking meetings that don’t fit?

Two things. The fee is flat, so we are not paid more for booking more. And you set the criteria in writing before we start. Only attended meetings that match them count toward the 15.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A prospect who fits your written AUM minimum, niche and geography, is a decision maker or co-decision maker, and attends live.

What does it cost?

The founding rate is $5,000 every 4 weeks. No setup fee and no per-meeting fees. The initial term is 3 billing cycles, about 90 days.

How do we know it’s worth the fee?

We work it out on the call with your numbers: your close rate on first meetings and your average new client. If the math doesn’t work for your firm, we’ll say so.

What do you need from us?

A kickoff call, access to your CRM, sign-off on your ideal client and the messages, and one advisor or owner on a short weekly call. Your advisors need room for the meetings.

How fast does it start?

We plan to launch within 30 days of kickoff, starting with your past prospects. The 90 days start at launch.

Do you promise new clients?

No. We commit to attended, qualified meetings. Closing them is yours.

Do you have RIA case studies?

Not yet. Advisor Booking is new to RIAs. That is why the founding rate is below standard pricing, and why the commitment is written into your agreement.
