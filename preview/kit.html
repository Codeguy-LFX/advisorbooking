Preview kit

Component sheet

One stylesheet for every preview page: preview/assets/kit.css. Sections 1 to 7 and the footer in 12 are copied from the home page. Sections 8 to 11 are new, drawn in the same language.

  • Link the embedded Geist first, then the kit. The kit imports nothing.
  • Every class starts with k-. Each block below lists its class names.
  • Night: put .k-night on the page or on any block. The right column shows it.
  • Quiz engine: put .k-quiz on the element around #apBody. The engine's own markup then takes these components (section 13).
  • States shown side by side use .is-hover .is-focus .is-pressed. They mirror the real pointer states.
