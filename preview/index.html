The few words that are new

The quiz wording did not change. The booking page did, because the live one tells people to answer more questions right after they answered six. These lines are taken from what your site already says about the call.

  • Heading: "Pick a time, [first name]." The live page says "Pick a time for your call, [first name]."
  • Line under it: "Your first step is a 20-minute call to see if your firm is a fit." Word for word from your home page. It replaces "Answer a few quick questions, then choose a time that works for you."
  • Your name and photo, with "20 min" and "Google Meet". No title and no biography. The photo is the one from your mailbox profile. Send a headshot if you want a different one.
  • What we cover: your ideal client and your list, your advisors' time and whether your firm has run outreach before, then whether this will work for your firm or not. Shortened from the sentence on your /pilot page.
  • Route B only: "Your call", "Your answers so far", "What you told us" and "Your call starts from these answers."
