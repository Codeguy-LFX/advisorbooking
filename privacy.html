Privacy Policy

How Advisor Booked collects, uses, and protects information from visitors and booking requests.

Effective date: [October 17, 2026]

Who we are

Advisor Booked is a marketing and appointment-setting services firm. The legal entity is De Jesus Ventures LLC, doing business as Advisor Booked, based in Boca Raton, Florida. This policy covers advisorbooked.com (the "site") and the information we collect through it. Questions go to hello@advisorbooked.com.

This policy does not cover data we handle for clients under a signed services agreement. That work is governed by the agreement and the client's own policies.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you book a 20-minute call, Calendly asks for your name, email, the time you pick, and anything you add to the prep question. We also record which page and campaign link you booked from. If you email us, we keep the email.

Scheduling data. If you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email, the time you pick, your time zone, and your answers to any prep questions. Calendly's own privacy policy also applies.

Usage data and cookies. Like most sites, we collect IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and time on page. We use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work and to measure traffic.

Advertising pixels. When enabled, the site loads the Meta Pixel and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. These tools set cookies and send Meta and LinkedIn information about your visit (pages viewed, browser data, and a hashed identifier where available) so we can measure our ads and show ads to people who have visited the site. Meta and LinkedIn use that data under their own policies.

UTM parameters. Links in our emails and LinkedIn messages carry campaign tags (UTM parameters). When you arrive through one, we record the tag with your visit and with any booking you make, so we know which message brought you here.

How we use it

We use the information above to:

  • Review your audit request and decide whether your firm is a fit.
  • Schedule, prepare for, and run calls with you.
  • Operate, secure, and measure the site.
  • Send service communications about a request or booking you made.
  • Show you retargeting ads on Meta and LinkedIn, if the pixels are enabled.
  • Meet legal and record-keeping obligations.

We do not use your information to make automated decisions with legal effect. We do not use your information to make automated decisions with legal effect.

How we share it

Service providers. We share information with vendors who process it for us: GoHighLevel (our CRM, which receives a booking notice by webhook with the page, campaign tags, and time of booking), Calendly (scheduling), our website hosting provider, our email provider, and the analytics and advertising platforms named above. They may use it only to provide services to us, except Meta and LinkedIn, which also act under their own policies.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information to comply with law, a court order, or a government request, or to protect our rights, our users, or the public.

Business transfers. If Advisor Booked is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

No sale. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for money. Loading advertising pixels may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some state laws. You can opt out as described below.

Cookies and opt-outs

  • Browser settings. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. Some parts of the site may stop working.
  • Meta. Manage ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences, or in your Facebook or Instagram settings.
  • LinkedIn. Manage ad settings at linkedin.com/psettings/advertising.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising. Email hello@advisorbooked.com with "opt out" in the subject line and we will exclude your data from retargeting audiences we control. How long we keep it

We keep booking records and call notes and call records in our CRM while we evaluate or work with your firm and for [3 years] afterward, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve a dispute. Usage and advertising data is kept according to each platform's retention settings, which we set to the shortest practical period. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, including access controls, two-factor authentication on our systems, and vendors with published security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop using it for marketing.

Email hello@advisorbooked.com. We will confirm your identity using the email address on file and respond within 45 days.

State privacy rights. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, Florida, and other states with consumer privacy laws may have additional rights, including the right to know, correct, and delete personal information, to opt out of targeted advertising and sales, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Advisor Booked is a small business and may fall below the thresholds that make these laws apply. We honor the requests above for every visitor regardless. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response, and we will answer within [45 days]. Florida's Digital Bill of Rights applies to larger controllers; we note it here because we are a Florida business. Email communications

If you book a call, we may send you emails about your request and, with your consent, marketing emails about our services. Every marketing email includes our physical address and an unsubscribe link, and we honor opt-outs within 10 business days as required by CAN-SPAM. Service emails about a booking you made are not marketing and do not have an unsubscribe link. We do not send marketing text messages. Children

The site is for professionals at registered investment advisers. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a minor has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top and, for material changes, post a notice on the site. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
