What happens on the call

  1. 1We look at where you are. Your CRM of past prospects, your current marketing and your goals.
  2. 2We run the first-meeting math. With your close rate and your average new client size, we work out your break-even cost per attended, qualified meeting. Rough numbers are fine.
  3. 3We show you the first 90 days. How reactivation and outreach would be set up for your firm, and what the 15-meeting commitment covers.
  4. 4You get a yes or a no. If we’re not a fit, you still leave with a clear plan for doing it yourself.
