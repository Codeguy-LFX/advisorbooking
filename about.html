About Advisor Booking

Advisor Booking is a marketing and appointment-setting company. We help independent financial advisory firms (registered investment advisers, or RIAs) get first meetings with prospective clients.

Who we are

Advisor Booking is operated by De Jesus Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company based in Boca Raton, Florida. We work with advisory firms across the United States.

What we do

We build and run the outreach that fills an advisory firm's calendar, so its advisors spend their time in meetings and not on prospecting. For each client firm we:

  • Build a list of business owners and high-net-worth individuals who match the firm's ideal client, and clean up the contacts already in the firm's CRM.
  • Write the emails and LinkedIn messages. The firm approves every message before anything is sent.
  • Send the approved messages by email and LinkedIn to new prospects and to the firm's past contacts.
  • Have a real person read and answer every reply, and check each person against the firm's written criteria.
  • Book qualified first meetings onto the advisors' calendars.
  • Report every Monday: messages sent, replies, meetings booked, meetings attended, and cost per meeting.

The outreach we run for client firms is by email and LinkedIn.

Who we work with

Independent RIAs with $50M to $250M+ in assets under management and 2 to 10 advisors.
