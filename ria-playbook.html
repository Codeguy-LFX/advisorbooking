Questions

Yes. Enter your email and we send it. You don’t have to book a call.

You take a 60-second fit check. If your firm looks like a fit, you pick a time for a 20-minute call on Google Meet. If not, we tell you right away. Either way, you get the Playbook.

No. We book attended first meetings for your firm only. Nothing is shared with another advisor.

Your compliance team approves every outreach message before it is sent. The compliance decision stays with your firm. The Playbook includes a checklist you can send them.
