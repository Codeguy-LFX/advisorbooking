Questions people ask

Plain answers. If yours is not here, ask on the call.

What do you actually do?

Three things, every week. We build a list of the right people. We send them a short, plain message your compliance officer has approved. We follow up until a real person answers. Then a real person on our team checks the reply and books the meeting on your calendar. We measure one thing: what each meeting cost.

Who is this NOT for?
  • Firms under $25M in assets, or firms that only charge by the hour
  • Firms with no list and no budget to build one
  • Firms whose compliance team says no to all outreach
  • Firms that want one campaign, not a system
  • Firms with no advisor who has time for 8 or more new meetings a month

These are not judgments. They are the conditions the system needs to produce meetings.

How do you handle compliance?

Every message goes to your compliance officer before it is sent. We never make claims about investment results. We never contact anyone for your firm without approved copy. Your compliance team keeps a copy of every message we send. If they say no to all outreach, we are not a fit, and we will tell you that on the call.

What does it cost?

Setup is $5,000 to $10,000, depending on whether we build your list or clean yours. After that it is a monthly fee plus a fee for each meeting that happens. You get exact numbers on the call, once we know your list and your goal.

Will we get a lot of bad meetings?

No. Before we start, you tell us what a good fit looks like: how much they have, what job they hold, and what is going on in their life. A real person reads every reply. People who do not fit get a polite no. You only pay the meeting fee when the person fits your rules and shows up.

We have tried marketing agencies before.

So have most of the firms we talk to. Agencies sell things like a campaign, a website, or ad views. We sell meetings that happen, and we report one number every week. If the number is bad, you see it in week two, not month six.

Do you have case studies yet?

Not yet. Advisor Booking is new. Each case study will be published when that client passes day 90, with the same numbers every time: meetings per month, show-up rate, and what each meeting cost. Nothing on this site will be a guess.

What do you need from us?

One kickoff call. Access to your CRM or your list. Your compliance officer reviewing messages within 5 business days. And advisors who show up to the meetings we book.
