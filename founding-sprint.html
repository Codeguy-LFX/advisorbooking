Why you're stuck

It isn't your advisors, and it isn't your service.

Firms your size usually stall for four reasons.

01Your growth runs on something you don't control.

Referrals bring your best clients, but they're lumpy. A strong quarter is followed by a quiet one. Your only lever is to "stay top of mind" and hope.

02Nobody owns prospecting.

Advisors are hired to advise, and ops is busy with ops. Outreach becomes "something we'll get to." It happens in a burst in January and is dead by March.

03Warm replies die in the inbox.

When outreach does go out, replies like "Maybe later," "What's this about?" or "Send me something" sit for three days. By the time someone answers, the moment has passed. Most outreach doesn't fail at the first message. It fails at the reply.

04Compliance friction kills momentum.

Every new message needs review. Nobody wants to be the person who sent something the CCO hadn't seen, so nothing gets sent.

What you've probably already tried

  • A marketing agency. You got content, a website refresh, social posts and a monthly report full of impressions. You still can't say whether any of it put a meeting on a calendar.
  • Seminars and events. They're expensive and slow, and half the room came for the dinner.
  • Bought leads. Form-fills that were often sold to several other firms the same afternoon.
  • A junior hire doing outreach. Prospecting was 20% of their job, so it got none of their attention.
  • Doing it yourself. You sent 40 LinkedIn messages one weekend. Then client work came back.

The pattern is the same every time: a tactic with no system behind it and nobody running it day to day.

What counts as an attended, qualified meeting

  1. The prospect meets the criteria you set in writing before launch, such as minimum investable assets, your service area, and not already being a client.
  2. We booked them onto an advisor's calendar.
  3. They actually attended the first meeting.

These don't count: no-shows, existing clients and anyone outside your written criteria. They don't count for us either.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will our compliance team allow outbound to HNW prospects?

That's your CCO's decision, so we build around it. Every message is approved before it's sent. Every reply is logged where your CCO can see it. Messages contain no performance claims, testimonials or investment advice. The CCO Ready Pack gives your CCO a draft outreach policy and a books-and-records checklist to adapt.

If your compliance team says no to outbound, we're not a fit, and we'll tell you so on the call.

What exactly is a "qualified" meeting?

A meeting with someone who matches the criteria you write into Schedule A, was booked by us and actually attended. You set the bar, in writing, before launch.

What happens if you don't hit 15?

We keep working at our cost, with no operating fee, until your team has taken 15 attended, qualified meetings. That holds as long as you've done your part on approvals, calendar availability and attendance.

You have no RIA case studies. Why should I trust you?

You shouldn't trust us blindly. That's why the founding price is half our standard rate, the guarantee is in the contract, and you see the real numbers every week from launch. If it isn't working, you'll know long before you've spent much.

Won't cold outreach make our firm look desperate?

Not the way we run it. Volume is low and messages are plain text in your firm's voice. They read like a note from an advisor, not an ad. Every email includes an opt-out, and dedicated sending domains protect your main domain.

We get plenty of referrals. Why do we need this?

Keep every one of them. This is a second source, not a replacement. The real question is whether you can predict next quarter's referrals. If you can, you may not need us.

We tried an appointment-setting firm before and got junk meetings.

Most setters book anyone who will say yes, because they're paid on volume. We're measured on attended meetings that match criteria you wrote down. No-shows and bad fits don't count toward our guarantee.

How much of our time does this take?

A 60-minute kickoff, CCO review time mostly in the first three weeks, then 20 minutes a week. Your advisors' job is to run the meetings.
