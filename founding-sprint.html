D Why you're stuck

It isn't your advisors, and it isn't your service.

Firms your size usually stall for four reasons.

01Your growth runs on something you don't control.

Referrals bring your best clients, but they're lumpy. A strong quarter is followed by a quiet one. Your only lever is to "stay top of mind" and hope.

02Nobody owns prospecting.

Advisors are hired to advise, and ops is busy with ops. Outreach becomes "something we'll get to." It happens in a burst in January and is dead by March.

03Warm replies die in the inbox.

When outreach does go out, replies like "Maybe later," "What's this about?" or "Send me something" sit for three days. By the time someone answers, the moment has passed. Most outreach doesn't fail at the first message. It fails at the reply.

04Compliance friction kills momentum.

Every new message needs review. Nobody wants to be the person who sent something the CCO hadn't seen, so nothing gets sent.

What you've probably already tried

  • A marketing agency. You got content, a website refresh, social posts and a monthly report full of impressions. You still can't say whether any of it put a meeting on a calendar.
  • Seminars and events. They're expensive and slow, and half the room came for the dinner.
  • Bought leads. Form-fills that were often sold to several other firms the same afternoon.
  • A junior hire doing outreach. Prospecting was 20% of their job, so it got none of their attention.
  • Doing it yourself. You sent 40 LinkedIn messages one weekend. Then client work came back.

The pattern is the same every time: a tactic with no system behind it and nobody running it day to day.

Why this matters now

Since the SEC Marketing Rule took effect, your CCO has to defend every claim your firm makes. Most agencies still sell vanity metrics and vague "brand" work, and you carry the risk.

Meanwhile, business owners sell their companies and HNW families review their advisors every week. They're taking first meetings with somebody.

The fix isn't another tactic. It's a system someone owns, with one number you watch every week and your CCO's approval built in.
