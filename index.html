    We rewrite one or two advisor profiles to match the outreach: a new headline, a tighter About focused on your niche and promise, and a clear book-a-call button, so profile views turn into booked meetings.

    From our in-house database we find the CPAs and attorneys around your geography and niche, then run campaigns to book compare-notes calls on mutual clients and be-our-go-to intro calls. A second pipeline: HNW clients through trusted professionals, not just cold outreach.

    Dedicated sending domains and inboxes, set up and warmed. Basic integration with your CRM and calendar so replies and bookings are tracked. A simple pipeline view: contacted, replied, qualified, booked, held.

  • 07

    Daily outbound and reactivation

    An agreed daily volume of email from your dedicated inboxes to the right segments. LinkedIn outreach from advisor profiles using approved scripts. Periodic reactivation pushes to dormant leads and past intro calls.

FAQ

What do you actually do?

The same work, every week. We build a list of the right people. We send them marketing on your behalf that you have approved. We follow up until a real person answers. Then a real person on our team checks the reply, qualifies the prospect, and books the meeting on your calendar. We nurture them so they show up. We build automations and a system that lets you track and convert meetings at the highest rate. We measure one thing: what each meeting cost.

Who is this NOT for?
  • Firms under $25M in assets, or firms that only charge by the hour
  • Firms with no CRM data and no budget to build a list of their ideal clients
  • Firms whose compliance team says no to all outreach
  • Firms that want one campaign, not a system
  • Firms with no advisor who has time for 8 or more new meetings a month

These are not judgments. They are the conditions the system needs to produce meetings.

What does it cost?

Setup is $5,000 to $10,000, depending on two things:

  1. Whether we build you a list, enrich your list, or both.
  2. How much marketing volume we will run, based on your advisor count and the size of your prospect list.

Once those numbers are agreed and setup is complete, there is a monthly fee plus a fee for each meeting that happens. You get exact numbers right after the demo call, once we know your list and your goal.

Will we get a lot of bad meetings?

No. Before we start, we ask you questions so you can tell us what a good fit looks like: how much they have, what job or situation they are in, where they are located, and what is going on in their life. A real person reads every reply. People who do not fit get a polite no. You only pay the meeting fee when the person fits your rules and shows up.

We have tried marketing agencies before.

So have most of the firms we talk to. Agencies sell things like a campaign, a website, or ad views. We sell what actually drives revenue: qualified meetings that happen, and the clients that come with them. We report one number every week. If the number is bad, there is no hiding. Our incentives are aligned with yours.

What do you need from us?

One demo call where we determine if we are a good fit. We work out who your ideal client is and how we will get them booked with you. We build you a list and/or you give us access to your CRM data so we can enrich your list. We transform your marketing and social pages with our proven systems and copywriting. Meetings get booked with your qualified dream clients. And your advisors show up with the biggest smile to the meetings we book.
