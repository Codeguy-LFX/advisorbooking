FAQ

Book intro call
How fast do meetings start?

We plan to launch within 21 days of kickoff, unless compliance review takes longer. Meetings book as replies come in. The guarantee’s 90 days start at launch, so review time never counts against it.

How does compliance work?

Your Compliance Team approves every message before it sends. We send from separate domains and keep a reply log your Compliance Team can access. The compliance decision stays with your firm. Questions about investments, performance, or fees go to your advisor.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A business owner or HNW individual who fits your agreed AUM minimum, niche, and geography, and attends the call live. You set those rules before we start. People who do not fit get a polite no.

Who owns the list and data?

You do. Domains, inboxes, lists, and messages live in your accounts, in your firm’s name. If you bring it in-house, your team keeps running it.
