About Advisor Booking

Advisor Booking is operated by De Jesus Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company based in Boca Raton, Florida. We work with independent financial advisory firms (registered investment advisers) across the United States.

For each client firm we build a list of prospective clients who match the firm's ideal client, write email and LinkedIn messages that the firm approves, send them, have a real person answer every reply, and book qualified first meetings onto the advisors' calendars. We report results every Monday.

We are a marketing and appointment-setting company, not an investment adviser. Read more about us or see how the service works.
