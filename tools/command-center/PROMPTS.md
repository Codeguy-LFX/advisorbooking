# The three prompts, rewritten for Advisor Booking

Your prospects are independent RIA principals who raised a hand (asked for the Past Prospect
Playbook, took the quiz, booked a call). The deal is the Founding Pipeline Sprint. The channel is
email, from nick@advisorbooking.com. Every prompt is read only: nothing is created, changed, sent,
scheduled or queued in GoHighLevel or Gmail.

---

## PROMPT ONE: PULL EVERY OPEN PROSPECT

Use the Advisor Booking GoHighLevel sub-account (location OlHSy0ie2GEU9CN03QfE) and the Gmail
bridge for nick@advisorbooking.com, read operations only.

Pull every open opportunity in the Sales pipeline. Also pull every contact who has a human
conversation on record but no opportunity, because most prospects are not in the pipeline yet.
Leave out my own test contacts (anything at advisorbooking.com, nick+ addresses, my own phone).

For each one, pull the contact (name, email, phone, firm domain), the full email and SMS history
from GoHighLevel and from Gmail, every note and every open task.

Work out the date of the last real message in either direction. Workflow emails ("Your call is
confirmed", "Here is why you may want to schedule", "NEW AB LEAD"), calendar invitations and
cancellations, and any bulk or campaign send do not count. If skipping them changes the day count,
say so in the row. If their words only appear quoted at the bottom of my reply, use them and say so.

Flag who spoke last. If the prospect wrote last and I never answered, mark it. That is more urgent
than a prospect who went quiet on me.

Table: every prospect with no human contact in 14 days, ranked by deal value (no opportunity =
"no deal in CRM", ranked after the ones with value), columns: name, email, stage, deal value, days
quiet, who spoke last, the last thing anyone said in their own words, and one line on why it stalled
from what is in the thread. Then the total dollar value in that column, and the single most urgent
one with one sentence on why.

Only what is in GoHighLevel and Gmail. Do not estimate, fill gaps or invent a reason. If something
is missing write "not in CRM" and move on. Flag any firm that looks outside the US.

## PROMPT TWO: DRAFT THE SAVES

Read only. Drafting, not sending.

Take the top three off that list. For each, first read the last several messages both ways, every
note, and write down what they actually asked for, plus any objection, budget, compliance or timing
signal, with the date.

Then draft one follow up email per person, in my voice:

- Open with their own words or the exact thing they asked for (CLOSER: Overview, Label).
- Exactly one sentence of value that passes the value equation: it raises their belief they will get
  what they asked for, cuts the time, or cuts their work. A feature is not value.
- The job is to restart the conversation. No ask for the call, the deposit, the agreement or the
  price.
- End with one no oriented question (Voss): "Have you set the past prospect idea aside for now?"
  Never guilt, never a yes ladder.
- Exactly one question mark. Two sentences maximum, greeting included in the first.
- No links, no attachments, no pricing, no calendar link, no emoji. Never "just following up",
  "circling back" or "touching base".
- Match how I actually write in that thread ("Hi Dana," / "Hey Mark,", full sentences, periods).
- One concrete detail from their thread: the playbook, the 7-day plan, the compliance checklist,
  their firm, their quiz answer. If it could go to anyone else, rewrite it.
- Advisor Booking rules: never promise clients, assets, AUM, revenue or performance; never use the
  word wealth; the only promise is attended, qualified meetings; email and LinkedIn only.

Output a table: name, the exact last thing they said and the date, the draft (with subject line,
"Re:" the thread's subject), and which principle it leans on.

Skip anyone who wrote stop, unsubscribe, remove me, not interested, or anything that reasonably reads
that way, or who carries a do not contact flag. Name them and why. Remind me to send during their
business hours, their time zone.

## PROMPT THREE: FIVE PEOPLE TO TALK TO TODAY

Build the panel at the top of the Command Center: five names, ranked in this order, explainable in
one sentence: whoever is waiting on me first, then deal value, then people who wrote back in their
own words, then days quiet; outside-US firms last. If fewer than five are past 14 days, fill the
panel with the people going quiet and say so in a line above it.

Each row: name, deal value, days quiet, who spoke last, one plain line on why they are on the list.
Click a row to open: their exact last words and date, the drafted email per prompt two, the
principle it leans on, and three buttons only: Copy this draft, Open in Gmail (compose window with
their address, subject and draft filled in), Write me another (a second draft, same rules).
Under the buttons: if Gmail does not open, copy the draft and email them yourself; nothing sends
on its own. Never show a sent state.

Under the panel: total deal value of the five, and the date and time of the last refresh.
Rebuild every weekday at 7am Eastern. If the five and their last messages have not changed since
the last run, say so and leave the panel as it is.
