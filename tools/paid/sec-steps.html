How it works when we run it

Three steps. Built, launched, then tuned every week.

  1. Step 1 · Weeks 1 to 2

    Build

    We build your list of ideal clients and clean up your CRM. We write the messages, and your compliance team approves every one before anything is sent.

  2. Step 2 · Weeks 3 to 4

    Launch

    We contact the people already in your CRM first. New prospects start getting messages the same week. A real person answers every reply and books the first meetings onto your calendar.

  3. Step 3 · Every week after

    Grow

    Every Monday you get one simple report: messages sent, replies, meetings booked, meetings attended, and what each meeting cost. If a number is off, we fix the list, the messages, or the volume.
