What happens after I enter my email?

A 60-second fit check: five questions and your contact details. Then you can pick a time for a 20-minute call on Google Meet. It ends with a yes or a no.

Do you sell leads?

No. We book attended, qualified meetings for your firm only. Nothing is shared with another advisor.

How does compliance work?

Your compliance team approves every outreach message before it is sent. Outreach is by email and LinkedIn only, from domains and inboxes in your firm’s name that your firm owns. We keep a reply log your compliance team can access. Questions about investments, performance, or fees go to your advisor. The compliance decision stays with your firm.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A prospect who fits your written asset minimum, niche and geography, is a decision maker or co-decision maker, and attends live.

What does it cost?

A flat fee with no per-meeting fees. We go through it on the fit call.

How fast does it start?

We plan to launch within 30 days of kickoff, starting with your past prospects. The 90 days start at launch.

Do you promise new clients?

No. We commit to attended, qualified meetings. Closing them is yours.

Do you have RIA case studies?

Not yet. Advisor Booking is new to RIAs, and we have no RIA clients yet. That is why the commitment is written into your agreement.
