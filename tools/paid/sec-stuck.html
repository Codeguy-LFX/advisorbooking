Why the calendar isn’t full yet

  1. 1

    Referrals are passive.

    They are your best source of clients, and you can’t schedule them. You wait.

  2. 2

    The CRM is full of people nobody works.

    Past prospects went quiet and nobody went back to them.

  3. 3

    The usual options are built around something else.

    Many agencies report on campaigns and impressions. Bought leads often go to several advisors at once. Software still needs someone on your team to run it. A vendor paid per meeting earns more by booking more.

What you need is simpler: a qualified person in front of your advisor on a set day.
