Our 90-day commitment

At least 15 attended, qualified meetings in your first 90 days from launch.

In your first 90 days from launch, we commit to at least 15 attended, qualified meetings that match your written criteria. If we’re short, we keep working at our cost (no operating fee) until you’ve taken 15 such meetings.

Written into your services agreement

The commitment depends on three things from you

  • Agree on your ideal client and the messages at kickoff
  • One advisor or owner on the weekly call
  • Follow the process we set for booked meetings

A qualified meeting is with a prospect who fits your written asset minimum, niche and geography, is a decision maker or co-decision maker, and attends live. You set the criteria in advance. The 90 days start at launch, so compliance review time does not count against them.
