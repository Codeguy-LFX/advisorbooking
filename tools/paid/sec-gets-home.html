Everything you get with the Advisor Pipeline system

Weeks 1 to 2

We build it

  1. 01

    Strategy and targets

    A 90-minute kickoff to agree on your ideal client, your area, and what counts as a qualified meeting. Then a monthly meeting target for each advisor, reported every Monday.

  2. 02

    New outreach lists, plus CRM cleanup and enrichment

    New lists of HNWIs and business owners who fit your ideal client. Your CRM de-duped and cleaned, with better emails and phone numbers added where available.

  3. 03

    A message pack you own

    Every email, LinkedIn message, follow-up, and reminder we send, written for your firm and kept in one doc your team owns.

  4. 04

    LinkedIn profile tune-up

    We rewrite one or two advisor profiles with a clear headline, a focused About section, and a book-a-call button.

  5. 05

    Email and tracking setup

    Separate sending domains and inboxes, warmed up before the first email goes out. Connected to your CRM and calendar so every reply and booking is tracked.

From week 3, every day

We run it

  1. 06

    Daily outreach

    Emails and LinkedIn messages go out every day to your new list and your past contacts, using only messages you approved.

  2. 07

    Referral campaigns to CPAs and law firms

    We contact CPAs and attorneys near you to set up intro calls about shared clients. A second source of HNWI clients beyond cold outreach.

  3. 08

    Reply handling and qualification

    A real person reads every reply, answers basic questions, and checks each person against your rules before booking.

  4. 09

    Booking, reminders, and no-show follow-up

    Qualified people go straight onto your advisors’ calendars with a short note on why you are meeting. Reminders go out before each call, and no-shows get a follow-up.

Every week

We report on it

  1. 10

    Monday report and weekly call

    Messages sent, replies, meetings booked, and meetings held, plus a 20-to-30-minute call to decide what to do more of and what to cut.

  2. 11

    You own everything

    Domains, inboxes, lists, and messages live in your accounts. If you bring it in-house, your team keeps running it.
