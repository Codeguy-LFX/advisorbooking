Who this is for, and who it’s not for

For

  • Independent RIAs
  • $50M to $250M+ AUM
  • With a CRM of past prospects and a compliance team open to email and LinkedIn outreach
  • With an advisor who has room for more first meetings

Not for

  • Firms under $50M AUM
  • Firms with no prospect list
  • Firms whose compliance team does not allow outreach
  • Anyone who wants to buy leads
