What do you actually do?

The same work, every week. We build a list of the right people. We send them marketing on your behalf that you have approved. We follow up until a real person answers. Then a real person on our team checks the reply, qualifies the prospect, and books the meeting on your calendar. We nurture them so they show up. We build automations and a system that lets you track and convert meetings at the highest rate. We measure one thing: what each meeting cost.

Who is this NOT for?
  • Firms under $25M in assets, or firms that only charge by the hour
  • Firms with no CRM data and no budget to build a list of their ideal clients
  • Firms whose compliance team says no to all outreach
  • Firms that want one campaign, not a system
  • Firms with no advisor who has time for 8 or more new meetings a month

These are not judgments. They are the conditions the system needs to produce meetings.

What does it cost?

For founding firms: $5,000 every 4 weeks, billed to a card on file. No setup fee and no per-meeting fees. The initial term is three billing cycles, about 90 days, then simple 4-week renewals.

Later clients pay our standard pricing, which starts with a $10,000 one-time setup fee. Founding firms keep their flat $5,000 every 4 weeks as long as they stay.

Will we get a lot of bad meetings?

No. Before we start, we ask you questions so you can tell us what a good fit looks like: how much they have, what job or situation they are in, where they are located, and what is going on in their life. A real person reads every reply. People who do not fit get a polite no. Only meetings that fit your rules and actually happen count toward the 15.

We have tried marketing agencies before.

So have most of the firms we talk to. Agencies sell things like a campaign, a website, or ad views. We sell what actually drives revenue: qualified meetings that happen, and the clients that come with them. We report one number every week. If the number is bad, there is no hiding. Our incentives are aligned with yours.

What do you need from us?

One demo call where we determine if we are a good fit. We work out who your ideal client is and how we will get them booked with you. We build you a list and/or you give us access to your CRM data so we can enrich your list. We tune your key advisor LinkedIn profiles and outbound messaging. Meetings get booked with your qualified dream clients. And your advisors show up ready for 8 or more new meetings a month.
