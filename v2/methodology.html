How the system works

Every stage feeds the next. By the time a prospect is on your calendar, they fit your written criteria, they’ve talked to a real person, and they’ve said yes to a meeting about moving or adding assets.

Step 1 · Week 1

Your written criteriaBefore anything sends

We agree on who your best clients are and what counts as a qualified meeting. Those criteria go in writing, in your services agreement, and every reply is checked against them.

  • Niche strategy and ideal client profile
  • Assets, niche and geography, in writing
  • A decision maker or co-decision maker
  • A meeting about moving or adding assets
  • Signed off by you in week 1

Step 2 · Week 1

Reactivation firstYour dormant CRM

The fastest first meetings come from people who already know you. We clean and enrich your CRM, map who to contact first, and work it from your own mailbox.

  • CRM cleanup and enrichment for 1,000+ contacts
  • A Dormant List Map of who to contact first
  • Short messages in your voice
  • Every message approved by your CCO first

Step 3 · By day 14 to 21

New outboundMatched to your best clients

We build new lists of business owners and HNW individuals who look like your best clients, then reach them by email and LinkedIn under your firm’s name.

  • New prospect lists for your niche and area
  • Domains and inboxes set up in your firm’s name
  • LinkedIn profile tune-up for your advisors
  • Email and LinkedIn only. No texts to prospects
  • Every message approved by your CCO first

Step 4 · Every day

A real person repliesWithin 1 business day

Every reply is read by a person, checked against your written criteria and answered. People who fit get booked. People who don’t get a polite no.

  • Every reply handled within 1 business day
  • Checked against your written criteria
  • A polite no for people who don’t fit
  • No bots answering your prospects

Step 5 · Every meeting

Booked, reminded, briefedSo meetings actually happen

Qualified prospects book onto your advisor’s calendar. Reminders go out before the meeting, and a no-show gets a follow-up. You get a one-page brief before each meeting.

  • Booking straight onto your calendar
  • Reminders and no-show follow-up
  • A one-page brief before each meeting
  • An Advisor Follow-Up Kit for after the meeting

Step 6 · Every week

One numberCost per attended, qualified meeting

Every week you get one report and one working session. If the number is off, we change the list, the messages or the volume before we ask you to spend more.

  • A weekly report and working session
  • A Day-60 review of meeting quality
  • Changes to list, messages or volume first
  • You own all domains, copy, templates and data

FAQ

How fast do meetings start?

Reactivation starts in week 1, from your own mailbox, with people already in your CRM. New outreach goes live once your domains are warmed and your CCO has approved the messages. We plan for that within 21 days of kickoff.

What does it cost?

It’s $5,000 every 4 weeks. No setup fee and no per-meeting fees. The first term is 3 billing cycles, about 90 days, then it renews every 4 weeks.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee meetings, not outcomes. Word for word:

“In your first 90 days from launch, we commit to at least 15 attended, qualified meetings that match your written criteria. If we're short, we keep working at our cost (no operating fee) until you've taken 15 such meetings.”

We never promise clients, assets or revenue. What happens in the meeting is up to your advisor.

How does compliance work?

Your CCO approves every message before anything goes out. Email and LinkedIn only, never texts to your prospects. Fees are flat, never tied to meetings or assets.

Will we get a lot of bad meetings?

No. Before we start, you tell us what a good fit looks like: assets, niche, location and what’s going on in their life. A real person reads every reply. People who don’t fit get a polite no. Only meetings that fit your written criteria and actually happen count toward the 15.

What do you need from us?

Three things. Sign off on your ideal client and the messaging in week 1. Have one advisor or owner on the weekly working session. Follow the process we set for booked meetings. Plus access to your CRM so we can clean it and work it first.
