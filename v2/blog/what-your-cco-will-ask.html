What your compliance officer will ask about outbound email, and the answers

Five questions come up every time. Here’s how we answer them, and what to bring to your CCO.

NDNick De JesusNovember 5, 2026

Most RIA owners like the idea of outbound email and LinkedIn. Then they think about their CCO.

That’s the right instinct. Your compliance officer should be in the loop from day one. The good news is that the questions are predictable. Here are the five we plan for, and the answers we give.

1. Who approves the messaging?

Your CCO does, before anything sends. Every email, every LinkedIn message and every follow-up goes to them first. Nothing goes out that they haven’t seen.

2. Which channels?

Email and LinkedIn only. We don’t text your prospects, and we don’t run ads.

3. Are fees tied to meetings or assets?

No. It’s a flat fee every 4 weeks. No per-meeting fees, no success fees and nothing tied to assets or revenue. That keeps the arrangement simple to review, and it means there’s no reason to book a meeting that doesn’t fit.

4. Who owns the list and the data?

Your firm does. Domains, inboxes, copy, templates and data are set up in your firm’s name and stay with you.

5. Any performance or return claims?

No. The messages never promise returns, performance or results. They invite a conversation, and that’s all.

What to bring to your CCO

  • The full sequence, every touch, in the order it sends
  • Who it goes to: past prospects, new lists, or both
  • The sender: which advisor’s name and mailbox
  • How replies are handled, and by whom
  • How someone asks to stop hearing from you, and how fast that’s honored
  • Where the record of what was sent is kept

Make approval quick

Send the full sequence at once, not one email at a time. Agree on a turnaround up front. Keep a simple log of what was approved and when. Short, plain messages are faster to review than clever ones.

This is general information, not legal or compliance advice. Your CCO and your firm’s policies have the final word.
