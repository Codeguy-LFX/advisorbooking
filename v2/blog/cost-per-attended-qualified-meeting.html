Cost per attended, qualified meeting: the one number to watch

Opens, clicks and booked calls all look good on a report. Only one number tells you if outreach is working.

NDNick De JesusNovember 7, 2026

Most marketing reports are full of numbers that feel like progress. Opens. Clicks. Impressions. Even booked calls.

None of them pay your advisors. A booked call that doesn’t show, or a meeting with someone who doesn’t fit, costs an advisor an hour and gives nothing back.

So we report one number every week: cost per attended, qualified meeting.

What counts

A meeting counts only if all of these are true:

  • The prospect fits your written criteria: assets, niche and geography
  • They’re a decision maker or co-decision maker
  • The meeting is about moving or adding assets
  • They show up, live

Your firm sets those criteria in writing before anything sends. If a meeting misses any of them, it doesn’t count.

How to work it out

Take everything you spent on outreach in a period. Divide it by the number of attended, qualified meetings in that same period. That’s the number.

Then compare it with what a first meeting is worth to your firm, using your own numbers: what a new client is worth to you in a year, and how often your first meetings turn into clients. It’s an estimate, not a promise, but it tells you quickly whether the number is healthy.

Why booked calls are the wrong number

Booked calls are easy to inflate. Loosen the targeting, book anyone who replies, and the number goes up while the calendar fills with people who will never move assets. Attendance and fit are where the real cost hides.

How our pricing is built around it

We charge a flat $5,000 every 4 weeks. No setup fee and no per-meeting fees. The guarantee, word for word:

In your first 90 days from launch, we commit to at least 15 attended, qualified meetings that match your written criteria. If we're short, we keep working at our cost (no operating fee) until you've taken 15 such meetings.

So over the first 3 cycles, the most you pay per attended, qualified meeting is $1,000: $15,000 divided by 15. Every meeting beyond 15 brings that number down.

What to do when the number is off

Don’t spend more first. Change the list, the message or the volume, in that order, and watch the number for a few weeks. That’s what the weekly working session is for.
