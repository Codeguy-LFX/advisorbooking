FAQ

Do you sell leads?

No. We book attended first meetings for your firm only. Nothing is shared with another advisor.

We’ve tried agencies before. Why is this different?

Many agencies sell a campaign and report on activity: sends, clicks and views. We are judged on one thing: attended, qualified meetings. You see one number every week, so if it isn’t working you know early, not six months in.

How does compliance work?

Your compliance team approves every outreach message before it is sent. Outreach is by email and LinkedIn only, from domains and inboxes in your firm’s name that your firm owns. The 90 days start at launch, so review time does not count against them. The compliance decision stays with your firm.

Per-meeting vendors booked us junk. What stops that here?

Two things. The fee is flat, so we are not paid more for booking more. And you set the criteria in writing before we start. Only attended meetings that match them count toward the 15.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A prospect who fits your written asset minimum, niche and geography, is a decision maker or co-decision maker, and attends live.
