Three funnels, side by side.

A and B rebuild two competitor funnels as captured, so we can study them. C is the Advisor Booking master we’ll ship. All three use the same form engine and the site’s look.

A · Study

Clients Blackbox pattern

Meta ad straight to a 5-question intake. Only Q1 routes. Booking page filters nothing; the booked and homework pages do the selling.

  1. Intake form (5 screens, Q1 “No” ends it)
  2. Booking page (banner + 8 fields, no logic)
  3. Booked page (4-step path, proof)
  4. Pre-call homework
  5. Emails + SMS: docs/funnel-a-emails-sms.md
B · Study

AAM pattern

The form is the landing page. 11 screens, every answer saved, routed in the browser to 4 booking tiers or an inline no.

  1. Application landing page (form on screen 1)
  2. Routes to Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, Triage, or an inline DQ
  3. Booked state: check email, join Meet, come prepared
  4. Emails + SMS: not captured
C · Master

Advisor Booking

Sales page, then an 11-screen application routed four ways, then iClosed, then the pre-call page.

  1. /pilot sales page

  4. /book?call=short (15 min) · /book?call=main (20 min)
  5. /booked pre-call page
  6. Emails + SMS: docs/funnel-c-emails-sms.md
